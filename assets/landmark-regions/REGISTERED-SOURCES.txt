The shared catalogue supplies 68 skull landmark objects. 67 geometry assets are
available; one alveolar-process asset and its transform return NOTFOUND.
The publisher supplies selection volumes, not target-specimen masks. Selections
are intersected with their own source bone first; the resulting surface footprint
and its actual selection contour are transferred onto native specimen triangles.

The explicitly named Anterior_Wall, Posterior_Wall and Medial_Wall meshes and
PPF_Region_v5 bone assembly use their full scene transform chains relative to Head.
Source scene descriptions identify the walls' maxillary, sphenoid and palatine
owners. These labels constrain a shared smooth deformation; each wall is matched
only to its owning bone's facing surface. A separate cavity volume is no longer
cropped by source-bone distance: that method lost the anterior and medial walls.
All three wall/bone pairs currently transfer on both sides of both viewer skulls.
This is bone coverage, not proof of complete or accurate anatomical boundaries.
One authored side is reflected for the opposite side; source laterality and all
cross-specimen transfers are estimates. Projection distances and deformation
checks remain geometric diagnostics, not an anatomical accuracy measurement.

Transfer method
Bone geometry initializes and constrains similarity ICP and regularized smooth
deformations. Native bone triangles are clipped to the transferred source footprint/outline.
Internal tessellation edges are excluded from its boundary. Mirrored target bone
winding is reconciled with supplied GLB normals, retaining vertices and face IDs.
BioDigital transfers must retain 95% of sampled projected source surface points
within one display unit; failures are withheld rather than replaced by blobs. No author surface is
rendered as a floating substitute for the target bone. Native source selections
and fitted opening rims take precedence over transferred estimates.
Source-file hashes, target-model hashes, fit residuals, deformation checks,
partial coverage and rejections are recorded in registered-regions.json.
Geometric validation does not establish anatomical accuracy. Cross-specimen
regions remain optional estimates; no 99% accuracy claim is supplied.

Bregma and lambda use native exterior three-bone junction fits, not independent
per-bone author volumes. All point landmarks use compact visual indications;
these are point locations, not supplied anatomical area boundaries.
