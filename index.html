About this model

Anatomy and sources

All 22 skull bones and the dental arches of a CT-scanned specimen, retained at full source detail.

Mesh and resolution

Landmarks and accuracy

Hover or select a landmark to preview it. Estimated regions are included and identified; their anatomical boundaries remain unvalidated.

Surface selections, transferred regions, fitted openings and contact bands, and point guides have different sources. A location guide has no painted anatomical boundary. Registration fits and triangle checks do not measure anatomical accuracy.

Landmark names and initial locations draw on Z-Anatomy / BodyParts3D and anatomical terminology. The CT supplies no named landmark boundaries; supplied landmark selections belong to the supplementary ear bones. Available mapped regions include BioDigital and UBC FossaFinder geometry, registered to this specimen. Label positions use bone-shape registration, native surface checks and supported source anchors; the selection card identifies each entry’s method and source.

Fossa highlights follow bony walls around a space. See-through previews reveal covered regions while retaining the same surface boundaries.

Sinuses and cavities

The six cavity groups preserve this specimen’s original segmented frontal, maxillary and sphenoidal sinuses, ethmoidal and mastoid air cells, and bony labyrinths. They can be shown independently; surrounding bones fade while a cavity is displayed.

Colours and highlights

Bones start in ivory. Hover temporarily shows their assigned colour; the colour dot makes it persistent. Landmark checkboxes retain highlights. Pins, names, location cues and see-through previews are optional.

Navigation and study views

Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and right-drag to pan. Right-click a bone to hide it. Selecting a bone or landmark preserves bone visibility. The bone and landmark searches filter their lists independently. Expand a landmark type and bone to browse its entries. Focus centres a landmark; Reveal shows its bones and hides the others, with an option to restore the skull view.

Left and right refer to the subject. Exploded view and jaw opening are study aids; separated positions and motion are illustrative.

Explosion separates parts without resizing them. Fit reframes the current view. Section planes leave open cut surfaces; Reset all restores the initial controls and visibility.
