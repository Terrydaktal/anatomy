Individual structure identities, the exact source commit and archive hash

Adaptations in this project: selection of skull, ossicle, tooth and hyoid
meshes; evaluation of existing source modifiers; application of original
world transforms; rotation of axes; conversion to millimetres; translation
to the skull centre; display colours; conversion to GLB. No decimation,
remeshing, invented bones, or source-surface smoothing was performed.

This is an educational reference atlas, not a scan of the brain project's
subject. Teeth are not counted as bones; all 28 source teeth are included,
and third molars (wisdom teeth) are absent. The hyoid is an optional
associated bone, not one of the 22 skull bones. Exploded positions and jaw
opening are display transformations, not measured motion or anatomy.
