Adaptation: an approximately 17.5 x 17.5 mm skull-vault surface patch from the published binary
bone mask. Native-spacing marching cubes, rectangular computational crop faces, rigid
orientation/centering, display colour and smooth vertex normals; no decimation or geometric smoothing.
All porosity comes from the source segmentation; no artificial trabeculae were generated.
Three interpretive educational pins mark approximate cortical-table/diploe locations;
the source contains no separate tissue labels for those layers.
This independent donor fragment is not registered to the full-skull models.
Exact source checksums, voxel crop, transform and extraction settings are in manifest.json.
