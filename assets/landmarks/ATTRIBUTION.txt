Descriptions and additional study annotations were written for this viewer.

Reference pins are projected source leaders, mirrored pins, actual source cavity centres or expressly curated study locations.
Source labels displaced more than 10 mm can use explicitly curated replacement positions; original leader points/distances remain recorded.
CT label locations use bone-shape registration, native cortex, supported native fits and available source-region anchors. Original study coordinates remain in initial_placement. These are not independently validated anatomical coordinates.
Accepted mapped-region label anchors retain BioDigital/UBC source URLs per placement; retain ../landmark-regions/REGISTERED-SOURCES.txt and publisher terms for those local derivatives.
Suture polylines are inferred continuous exterior reference bone-contact locations, not separately segmented fibrous joints. They are omitted on CT.
No bony microstructure or missing canal walls have been synthesized. See catalogue.json coverage for limits and the separate micro-CT study.
