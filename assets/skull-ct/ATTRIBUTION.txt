Specimen: dry human skull OUVC 10503. CT: GE LightSpeed Ultra Multi-Slice,
625 micrometre slice thickness, 120 kV, 200 mA. These are acquisition
parameters, not a claim that every exported surface feature is accurate
to 625 micrometres.

SOURCE AND CONVERSION
The highest-resolution public 3D PDF is the source. The exact URL and
SHA-256 are recorded in manifest.json. Its U3D geometry is decoded using
the pinned svhss/u3d-mesh-extract decoder, an independent implementation
of Intel's U3D reference algorithm; the decoder is downloaded separately
into the ignored source cache, rather than redistributed with this app.
https://github.com/svhss/u3d-mesh-extract

All source triangles of the 22 skull bones and two dental arches are
retained. The two inferior nasal conchae are separate disconnected
components of one source resource; they are separated without changing
their surfaces. No other bones are synthesized or added from a different
specimen. Teeth remain grouped into the two original dental-arch meshes.
This specimen's export does not provide separately segmented auditory
ossicles or a hyoid. The viewer adds seven supplementary atlas bones from
../skull-associated/bones.glb with explicitly estimated placements; they
are not part of this native CT segmentation. See that asset's attribution.

Adaptations: lossless resource/component selection, replacement of the
shared PDF presentation rotation with upright display axes, uniform display
scaling, centering, smooth surface normals, optional study colours and GLB
export. No decimation, subdivision, geometric smoothing or synthetic pores.
The original skull.glb remains the stable native registration source.
surface.glb restores the PDF's original per-corner normals and authored
normal seams. Its triangle corners, triangle order and face IDs are exactly
unchanged; this improves source shading fidelity without increasing scan
resolution or adding new anatomical detail. scripts/export_ct_surface.py
decodes that normal stream using the pinned decoder's bitstream primitives.
The U3D contains an invalid zero unit-scale field. Display coordinates are
not calibrated millimetres and must not be used as physical measurements.
